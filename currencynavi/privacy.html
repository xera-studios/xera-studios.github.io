CurrencyNavi privacy policy

Effective 1 November 2026. Publisher: Xera Studios. Contact: xera-studios@outlook.de.

Local app storage

CurrencyNavi stores entered amounts, selected currencies, ordering, check inputs, language, appearance, tutorial progress and downloaded reference rates in local app storage. It has no account system, analytics SDK, advertising SDK, app-operated cloud synchronization or subscription service. The app does not upload your entered amounts or exchange offers.

Exchange-rate requests

Downloading rates needs internet. The app contacts api.frankfurter.dev. If a request fails, it may contact cdn.jsdelivr.net and latest.currency-api.pages.dev for the open Currency API dataset. Requests contain the currency codes required for conversion, not monetary inputs. These services and network infrastructure receive technical information such as IP address and request time and may process it under their own policies. CurrencyNavi does not use these requests for advertising or tracking.

Offline use and operating-system backups

The last successful rate download remains usable offline. Operating-system backups may include local app storage according to device settings. CurrencyNavi does not run its own backup server. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes the local data; restoring an operating-system backup may restore settings. We do not receive a copy of your saved inputs.

Permissions and purchases

Internet access is used to update rates. The app does not request contacts, camera, microphone or location access. There is no login, advertising tracking or in-app purchase flow.

Support correspondence

If you email support, we receive the address and information you choose to send. Do not include passwords, payment-card details or sensitive financial information. Correspondence is used to answer the request; your email provider and our email provider process delivery information.

Children and updates

CurrencyNavi is a general-purpose calculator and does not knowingly request personal information from children. This policy and store disclosures will be updated if the app's data practices change.
