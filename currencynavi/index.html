One amount. Your currencies.

CurrencyNavi combines a multi-currency converter with an exchange-rate check. Enter one amount and see its value in your chosen currencies. Country flags make your list easy to scan; add, reorder or swipe away currencies as needed.

CHECK AN EXCHANGE OFFER
Choose the currencies you give and receive. Enter an offered rate and an optional fee, or enter both actual amounts without a rate. See the difference from the saved market reference in both currencies and as a percentage. Red and green result blocks, clear labels and minus signs make losses and gains easy to understand. Reverse the quote direction when needed.

READY WITHOUT INTERNET
Download rates once, then convert using the last successful update. Your amounts, currency list and settings stay on your device. Refreshing rates needs an internet connection.

A SIMPLE MOBILE TOOL
Light and Dark modes, a short introduction and 11 interface languages. No account, ads, subscriptions or analytics.

Reference rates are indicative and may differ from a bank, card provider, ATM or exchange desk. CurrencyNavi calculates comparisons; it does not transfer money or execute exchanges. Examples shown in store images are illustrative.
