Using the converter

Choose the top currency in the upper-right corner and add the currencies you need. Enter the amount at the top or tap any listed amount to edit it in that foreign currency. All other values, including the top amount, update from the last entry. Tap a row’s flag/code/down-chevron control to replace its currency. Already listed currencies are excluded. Edit reorders or removes rows; swipe deletes a row and Undo restores a recently removed row.

Checking an offer

Open Conversion check and choose From and To first; they must be different currencies. Enter what you give. Exchange desk accepts a rate in the receiving currency by default and an optional fee in the giving currency. The rate direction control inverts the quote. No rate accepts the final actual amount received after all fees.

Why rates differ

Saved daily reference rates are indicative. Banks, cards, ATMs and exchange desks may apply other rates, spreads or fees. The result compares an offer with the saved reference; it does not guarantee a transaction result. CurrencyNavi does not transfer money or execute exchanges.

Offline rates

Connect once to download rates. The last successful update stays cached. Failed updates preserve it. Check the displayed source and update details before relying on a comparison.

Language, appearance and decimals

Settings contains language, Light/Dark choice, introduction and licenses. The saved app theme is independent of device theme. Enter decimals with a point or comma, without thousands separators.

Saved data

Inputs and settings stay in app storage. The operating system may include them in its backups. See the privacy policy.
