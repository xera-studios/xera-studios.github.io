WalkMetric Privacy Policy

Effective September 13, 2026

In short: WalkMetric does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. Your step and fitness information stays on your device.

Health and fitness data

With your permission, WalkMetric reads step-count data from Health Connect on Android or Apple Health on iOS. The app uses this information only on your device to show your current steps, history, goals, distance estimates, walking-time estimates, statistics, and home-screen widget.

WalkMetric does not write health data, send health data to Xera Studios or third parties, use it for advertising, or combine it with other data.

Other data

WalkMetric has no user accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking, cloud storage, or social features. Goal and appearance preferences are stored locally on your device. The app does not request your precise location.

Your choices

You can deny or revoke step access at any time in Health Connect or Apple Health. You can remove locally stored app data by uninstalling WalkMetric.

Children

WalkMetric is a general-audience fitness utility and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version and effective date will be published on this page.
